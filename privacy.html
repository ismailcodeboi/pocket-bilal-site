Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Pocket Bilal does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to calculate prayer times for your area. Calculations happen entirely on your device. Your location is never sent to us or to any third party. To show your city name, the app uses Apple's built-in location service, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can instead enter your location manually in Settings.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service or server is used.

Calendar

If you turn on Calendar Integration, the app creates a "Pocket Bilal Prayers" calendar on your device and adds prayer time events to it. The app does not read or upload your other calendar events.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising, and does not require an account.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
